The Heart of the Village

Gram Panchayat
Phulera

Nestled in the dusty bylanes of Uttar Pradesh, the Gram Panchayat Phulera office is far more than a government building — it is the beating heart of village life. Notices peel off its walls, bureaucracy sleeps in its files, and yet every problem in Phulera finds its way here.

For Secretary Abhishek Tripathi, it became an unlikely home. For Pradhan ji, a stage. And for the people of Phulera, their only window to the world outside.

The Story

Aap logon ke liye office hai, mere liye toh ghar hai.

— Abhishek Tripathi, Gram Sachiv  ·  Phulera, District Ballia

Panchayat is a TVF original series streaming on Amazon Prime Video. Set in the fictional village of Phulera in Uttar Pradesh, it follows an engineering graduate thrust into rural government service. What begins as a reluctant posting slowly becomes a story of belonging — of finding purpose, friendship, and humanity in the most unexpected of places. With gentle humour, deeply human characters, and an honest portrayal of rural India, Panchayat has become one of the most beloved Indian web series of the streaming era.
